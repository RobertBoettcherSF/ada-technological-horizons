    Intellectronics · Return to Earth

    What does persistent doubling imply?

    A normalised starting index of 1 and a 15-year doubling time follow a historical discussion of scientific periodicals. Ada computes unrestricted exponential growth. That is the scenario, not a forecast, and the starting 1 is not a historical count.

    N = N₀ · 2^(t / d)

    The factor is 2^(t / d) even when the starting quantity is zero. The quantity is then zero.

    Technical demonstration

    Add two numbers

    These two numbers are already filled in. Change either one and the sum updates. Ada does the addition. This is a boundary test, not a chapter calculator.

    Result

    Use a dot as the decimal separator in every language. Ada receives the binary64 value, not translated text.

    Contract: finite binary64 addition. A negative finite operand is an ordinary value. A non-finite operand or a non-finite sum is an error. Underflow to a finite result, including zero, is success. Tolerance for the published integer fixtures is exact binary64 equality.
